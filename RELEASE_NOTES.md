# v1.8.2

A quiet update, mostly for the office side: a new Employee Portal for every staff member, a forgot-password option on the staff sign-in page, and easier staff and login management for admins. Counters can take it at their next safe moment — nothing changes in the database, and billing works exactly as before.

## Staff sign-in

- **Forgot password?** Staff can now reset their own password from the sign-in page with a code sent to their email.
- **Counter PIN from your profile.** Set or reset your counter PIN yourself from the Security tab.

## Employee Portal

- A new **My Portal** page (Office → Me) shows each staff member their own career details first — work, attendance, leave and pay, month by month — with their social profile at the end.

## Staff and logins (for admins)

- **Admin → Users** and the **Staff Directory** now use the same add, edit and delete, and the permissions box saves properly again.
- A staff profile's **Access** tab edits the login right on the page. **Role** and **Reports to** have moved to the **Job** tab.
- New staff get a company email address (name@networkservice.in) automatically, and admins can set where anyone's company mail is forwarded. Staff can set their own forwarding from their profile.
- The super admin now shows on every HR screen.
- Chrome no longer fills a saved password into the Add/Edit User box by mistake.

## Accounts

- In the voucher registers, **right-click a Voucher No or Party name** to open it in a new window.
- A staff member's salary ledger can no longer be deleted from the chart of accounts by mistake.
- A voucher can no longer be saved even a paisa out of balance.

## Menu

- **POS Register** stays visible in the menu after being renamed and has its own icon; POS Shift has its own place in the menu too.
