# v1.16.1 [urgent]

Every counter should take this update today. This release brings a cleaner WhatsApp-style chat experience in CRM Messages with color-coded business phone lines, automatic ticket routing menus for customers, direct voucher opening from Bank Reconciliation, and fixes for desktop shortcuts on update. Recommended for office and counter users.

## WhatsApp & CRM Messages
- **Cleaner WhatsApp Chat UI**: Chat bubbles now match native WhatsApp styling with clear timestamps, delivery ticks, and distinct colors for each business phone number.
- **Customer Ticket Menu & Numeric Auto-Reply**: Customers messaging in on WhatsApp can choose an open repair or support ticket by replying with numbers (e.g. 1, 2) to link their conversation directly.
- **Create Deal / Support Ticket**: Staff can create a new Deal Lead or Help Desk ticket directly from an active WhatsApp customer chat or link existing tickets.
- **Auto-Assign on Reply**: Replying to a customer message automatically assigns the thread to you.
- **Customer Quick Profile Sheet**: Tap on customer avatars in WhatsApp messages to view profile info, balance, and recent history.
- **Mobile WhatsApp Inbox**: Full customer WhatsApp chat screen and threaded messaging inside the staff mobile app.

## Accounts & Bank Reconciliation
- **Open Vouchers from Bank Reconciliation & Ledger**: Click on entries in the Bank Reconciliation or general ledger to view the voucher directly (or right-click to open in a new window).
- **Preserved Sequence & Balances**: Fixed statement sequencing and daily closing balance display in bank reconciliation.
- **Date Boundary Highlights**: Clear visual markers showing statement date boundaries.

## Desktop App & Installer
- **Desktop Shortcut Fix**: Fixed an issue where desktop shortcuts were removed during automatic background updates. The shortcut now stays on the desktop reliably.
