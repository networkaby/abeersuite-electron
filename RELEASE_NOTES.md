# v1.16.22

A small update for the Campaigns screen. Install it at a convenient time — no need to stop billing for it.

## Campaigns — connecting Facebook and Google

- **Connect from the counter app**: Pressing Connect on the Facebook or Google card inside the counter app now opens the office site in your browser to finish sign-in, instead of getting stuck inside the app.
- **Business-owned Facebook pages**: Pages that belong to a Meta business portfolio now show up in the page list when you connect Facebook.
