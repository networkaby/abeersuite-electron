# v1.17.17

Every counter must install this. Sale bills now show the warranty of each item, and billing and saving are safer. Update the counters first, then the office. Until a counter is updated, its sync may pause once the office is updated.

## Warranty

- Each warranty term now has a type: Manufacturer, Seller, Combined (brand warranty plus the shop's own for the first part) or No warranty. All your old terms are marked Manufacturer.
- A sale bill remembers the warranty each item was sold with, so a reprint shows the same warranty later.
- A4 and thermal bills have a new Warranty section. You can change its wording, or hide it, in the print template.
- A term already used on a bill cannot be changed. Make a new term for a new policy.

## Billing

- A sale is saved a little faster.
- A quick second press can no longer post, cancel, restore or return a bill twice, or close a shift twice. Sale returns and gift cards are checked again while saving.
- A sale that has a saved return cannot be cancelled or deleted.
- Day Book now has Restore in its menu.

## Printing

- Thermal slips stop at the cutter, without a long blank tail, and there is a small gap under the barcode.

## Customer messages

- A counter that saves straight to the live database now sends WhatsApp itself. App and email messages are sent by the office, and can take up to about 30 minutes.
- If WhatsApp cannot confirm a message was sent, it is not sent again from another number. Staff are told to check first.
- App or email messages stuck at "pending" are tried again automatically.

## Customers and saving

- Adding a new person or company no longer says "Could not create" when it was saved.
- Vouchers, shifts, tickets, prices, staff, products, campaigns and contact details now show what was just saved.

## Products (online shop)

- AI notes are now one list of problems, with a tick on each. A doubtful photo has Keep and Remove, and a removed photo can be put back.
- The in-app product AI is hidden for now.

## Phone app

- The product photo screen shows the shop stage and which photos still need to be taken. Other fixes are listed separately, to do on the computer.
