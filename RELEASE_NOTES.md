# v1.17.12

Online shop and product-preparation update. Nothing changes at the billing counter, but every counter should install it now.

## Online shop products (office)

- The Products list now shows the stages as a numbered bar (Unpublished → Draft → Ready → Published) with a short "what next" line. Pending sits inside Published, and Attention shows in red.
- Bulk buttons show only the moves that make sense for the tab you are on, coloured by where the product will go. Bulk Publish is only on the Ready tab.
- Staff can flag a Published or Pending product for Attention, with an optional reason.
- AI notes end with a "Next step" line staff can press.
- A product missing a must-have photo can be given a temporary pass to publish; the photo is still asked for and the pass clears once it is added.
- Product edit page: Start button on Unpublished and Draft products, "Save and start" when there are unsaved edits. Badge, Stock and Shipping weight sit on one line; stock shows in the read-only Price, MRP & stock card. The low-stock floor is removed — Reorder handles it.

## Shop website

- "Show interest" on an out-of-stock product now creates a Deal for staff to follow up. It is refused for products the shop can already sell.
- Backorder products look like any other product; quick delivery (today / tomorrow) is hidden when the cart has a backorder item.
